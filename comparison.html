Player Comparison

This is the RCL Player Comparison! Here's how it works :) ↓↓↓

For starters, this is based on gab's player comparison, just automatically updating based on current RCL data. As such, points are calculated by taking a person's
top 15 completions, adding their placements together, adding any penalties (Total + 100) and subtracting 120 (perfect top 15), meaning the best score is a 0. Lastly,
whatever the tier of their hardest completion, they will have that rank rather than how ranks typically work, better reflecting on their skill whether they have a low score
or not.

This page is unfinished because I will likely be adding this to the profiles or accounts page once that's implemented, but I figured it'd still be cool to have until then.
